Step 2 • Research

The Most Predictable Path to YouTube Growth

Create clear videos that answer specific questions people are searching for. That’s it. Not random topics you want to talk about. Not guesses. Verified questions that real people type into YouTube’s search bar every single day.

86% of US viewers say they use YouTube to learn new things. If you teach, you grow.

Before ASQ

After ASQ

Verified topics, strategic titles, answering what people search for

Your Research Toolkit
Step 1 • Smart Creators Use Smart Tools
research_tools.sh — bash
# TOOL 1: YouTube Search Bar (Free, instant)
$ youtube_search "how to get started in video production"
> video production for beginners
> video production tips
> video production equipment
# Top = most searched. Bottom = least searched.
# Pro tip: use underscore + letter before your keyword
$ youtube_search "_ lose weight" # reveals hidden gems
# TOOL 2: Answer The Public (3 free searches/day)
$ open answerthepublic.com
> Enter 1-2 words → "bass fishing"
> Select: Google | YouTube | TikTok | Amazon
> Result: mind map of actual questions people ask
> "What bait attracts bass most?"
> "What is the number one bass lure?"
> "What is the trick to catching bass?"
# TOOL 3: AI Title Generator (ChatGPT/Claude)
